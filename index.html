Expert Knowledge

Dermatologist Skin Type Guide

Learn how to accurately diagnose your skin type, avoid counter-productive ingredients, and master an effective AM/PM routine.

Dry Skin Regimen & Blueprint

Ingredients to Embrace:
  • Multi-molecular Hyaluronic Acid
  • Bio-identical Ceramides (1, 3, 6-II)
  • Cold-pressed Squalane & Shea Butter
  • Glycerin and Panthenol (Pro-Vitamin B5)
Ingredients to Avoid:
  • High alcohol-based toners (denatured alcohol)
  • Harsh foaming sulfates (SLS / SLES)
  • High concentration AHA acids without moisturizer
  • Synthetic fragrances and eucalyptus oils
Recommended AM / PM Daily Protocol:

AM: Rinse with lukewarm water -> Botanical Hydrating Cleanser -> 15% Vitamin C Serum -> Barrier Cream -> Mineral SPF 50.
PM: Oil Cleanser -> Barrier Cream -> Overnight Hydrating Balm.

Oily & Shiny Skin Regimen

Ingredients to Embrace:
  • 2% Salicylic Acid (BHA pore refiner)
  • Niacinamide (reduces oil excretion)
  • Zinc PCA (antimicrobial sebum balance)
  • Oil-free hyaluronic gel creams
Ingredients to Avoid:
  • Heavy coconut oil or mineral oil balms
  • Over-cleansing with high-pH bar soaps
  • Skipping moisturizer (triggers rebound oiliness)
Recommended Protocol:

Use clarifying gel cleanser twice daily, followed by Niacinamide serum and matte water gel.

Sensitive & Reactive Skin Regimen

Ingredients to Embrace:
  • Centella Asiatica (Cica extract)
  • Bisabolol & Allantoin
  • Colloidal Oatmeal
Ingredients to Avoid:
  • Strong scrubs with walnut or apricot kernels
  • Essential oils (Citrus, Lavender, Peppermint)
  • High acid peels
Recommended Protocol:

Keep routine under 3 minimal products. Stick to fragrance-free ceramide formulations.

Acne & Texture Regimen

Ingredients to Embrace:
  • Encapsulated Salicylic Acid
  • Azelaic Acid 10%
  • Green Tea Polyphenols
Ingredients to Avoid:
  • Comedogenic waxes and heavy silicones
  • Over-exfoliating every single day
Recommended Protocol:

Nightly treatment with BHA clarifying mask + calming moisture barrier cream.

    The Science of Barrier Repair: Why Ceramides Are Non-Negotiable

    By Dr. Elena Vance, Lead Chemist • August 2026 • 5 min read

    The stratum corneum is composed of corneocytes surrounded by an intercellular lipid matrix consisting of approximately 50% ceramides, 25% cholesterol, and 15% free fatty acids. When this delicate ratio is compromised by harsh surfactants, extreme weather, or aggressive exfoliants, trans-epidermal water loss (TEWL) accelerates exponentially.

    How Bio-Identical Ceramides Restore Balance

    Our ceramide complex utilizes bio-identical stereoisomers of Ceramide NP, AP, and EOP that directly integrate into the lipid bilayer, creating an impermeable barrier against atmospheric pollution and transepidermal hydration loss.

    Key Takeaway:

    Always pair topical humectants (like Hyaluronic Acid) with occlusive ceramide creams to lock hydration inside the dermis.
